# Live test: hearing-aid microphone uplink

**Date:** 2026-10-29  
**Device:** Google Pixel 7 with paired LE Audio hearing aids  
**Desktop:** lilred running Linux/PipeWire  
**Transport:** encrypted relay over loopback using ADB reverse forwarding

Android reported: “Connected · selected microphone route verified by Android.” The desktop played a generated 1 kHz, 600 ms tone through its speakers while the phone relayed the selected hearing-aid microphone input. The tone was encoded at roughly 30% digital full scale.

The 30-second uplink capture contained a matching waveform at approximately 9.1248 seconds. The normalized waveform correlation was **0.60356** against the known tone. The desktop received 960,000 PCM samples (30 seconds at 32 kHz mono), with an observed uplink peak of 23,852. Together with the verified Android route and timed acoustic playback, this confirms that the test tone reached the relay from the selected hearing-aid microphone on this setup.

This single test does not measure speech quality, both-ear behavior, long-term stability, or compatibility with other hearing aids and phones. It did not test direct LAN, physical USB, or the Windows client. The capture and generated tone are retained only in a private local test directory, not in this repository.
